About

An independent cybersecurity partner for Moroccan businesses.

JectarOne helps small and medium-sized organizations across Morocco understand their real security risks and fix the ones that matter — without fear, jargon, or unnecessary spend.

Who we are

JectarOne is a specialist cybersecurity consultancy based in Morocco. We work with accounting firms, law firms, clinics, manufacturers, e-commerce businesses, software companies, and local startups — the organizations that keep the economy running but rarely have a dedicated security team.

Our role is simple: give business owners and IT managers an honest, clear picture of where they stand, and a realistic plan to improve. We are vendor-neutral, so our recommendations are based on what your business actually needs — not what we are trying to sell.

What we believe

Good security is practical, proportionate, and understandable. A report nobody reads protects nothing. We focus on findings you can act on, explained in language your whole team can follow.

How we work

  • Confidential by default. Every engagement is covered by a non-disclosure agreement. Findings are shared only with the people you authorize.
  • Business-focused. We prioritize risks by their impact on your operations, customers, and budget.
  • Remote and on-site. We adapt to your location and schedule across Morocco.
  • Transparent. You always know what we are doing, why, and what comes next.

Our values

Principles that guide every engagement.

  • Business-focusedAdvice tied to your operations and budget, not generic checklists.
  • Clear reportingExecutive summaries for leadership and technical detail for your team.
  • Local understandingWe understand how Moroccan organizations operate.
  • IndependentVendor-neutral guidance with no hidden agenda.
