FAQ

Answers for business owners and IT managers.

Have a more specific question about your environment? Reach out and we will give you a straight answer.

Ask a question
What does a security assessment involve?

We review your systems, networks, and key applications to identify weaknesses, then deliver a clear report with prioritized, business-focused recommendations you can act on.

Do you work remotely or on-site?

Both. Many engagements are delivered remotely, and we also work on-site for internal network audits and assessments that require physical access across Morocco.

How long does an engagement take?

It depends on scope, but most assessments for SMEs are completed within one to three weeks, from discovery to final report.

Can you help us prepare for ISO 27001?

Yes. We help you understand the requirements, assess current gaps, and build a practical roadmap toward ISO 27001 and security best practices.

Is our information kept confidential?

Every engagement is covered by a non-disclosure agreement. Findings and reports are shared only with the people you authorize.

What is the difference between a vulnerability assessment and penetration testing?

A vulnerability assessment identifies and prioritizes known weaknesses across your systems using scanning and expert review — broad coverage, lower cost. A penetration test goes further: our specialists actively exploit weaknesses to prove real business impact and show how far an attacker could get. Most SMEs start with a vulnerability assessment, then use penetration testing on their most critical systems.

How much does a security assessment cost for an SME?

Pricing depends on the size of your environment and the scope you choose. We keep engagements sized for small and medium businesses and give you a fixed, transparent quote before any work begins — no open-ended hourly billing. Contact us for a free scoping call and estimate.

Do you help us fix the issues after the assessment?

Yes. The report is only the start. We provide prioritized, practical remediation guidance your team can follow, and we can support or verify the fixes with a re-test to confirm the risks are closed.

Do you comply with Moroccan data protection requirements?

Yes. We handle client data in line with Moroccan data protection law (Law 09-08 and CNDP guidance). Engagement data is minimized, access-controlled, and returned or securely destroyed after the project on request.
