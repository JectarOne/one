Restaurant SEO Morocco: How to Get More Reservations from Google (2026)

Every night, thousands of Moroccans search Google Maps for "restaurant près de moi" or "أحسن مطعم كازا." The restaurants that capture this traffic are full. The ones that don't are empty — even with better food. This guide shows you exactly how to rank higher, get more clicks, and convert searches into reservations and orders.

76% of restaurant searches lead to a visit within 24 hours
5× more walk-ins for restaurants in Google Maps top 3
2,700% more direction requests for profiles with 100+ photos
90% of Moroccan food discovery happens on Google or Instagram

How Moroccans Discover Restaurants in 2026

The way Moroccans find restaurants has fundamentally shifted. Walk-in discovery from street presence alone is fading — especially for lunch spots, family outings, and tourist dining. Today, the decision happens before the customer leaves home:

  • Google Maps — "restaurant Maarif", "pizza livraison Rabat", "مطعم قريب مني"
  • Instagram — food photography, location tags, stories
  • TripAdvisor — international tourists and reviews-focused locals
  • Glovo / InDrive / other delivery apps — for takeaway and delivery
  • WhatsApp — group recommendations, direct reservations

Of these, Google Maps delivers the highest-intent traffic — people who are hungry, nearby, and ready to spend. This guide focuses on dominating Google Maps, then covering the supporting channels.

The Local Pack reality: When someone searches "restaurant [city]" on Google, the first three results — the Local Pack — capture ~70% of all clicks. Position 4 and below share the remaining 30%. If you're not in the top 3, you're essentially invisible to most searches.

Google Business Profile: The Restaurant Owner's Most Powerful Tool

Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is what appears when someone finds your restaurant on Maps or searches your name. It controls your photos, hours, contact info, menu, reviews, and booking options. Most Moroccan restaurant owners set it up once and forget it — which is why the gap between optimized and unoptimized profiles is enormous.

Choose the Right Category

Don't just pick "Restaurant." Google has highly specific categories that rank better for specific searches:

  • Moroccan cuisine → "Moroccan Restaurant"
  • Pizza → "Pizza Restaurant" (not just "Restaurant")
  • Café and light meals → "Café" + "Sandwich Shop"
  • Fine dining → "Fine Dining Restaurant"
  • Fast food → "Fast Food Restaurant"
  • Fish/seafood → "Seafood Restaurant"

Add your primary category plus 3–5 secondary categories that describe your full offering. A Moroccan restaurant that also does catering and has a café section should include all three as categories.

Add Your Full Menu to Google

Google lets you add a menu directly in GBP — either as a link to a menu URL or by manually entering items. This is one of the most underutilized features in Morocco and one of the highest-impact for ranking.

When you list "Tagine de poulet", "Couscous royal", "Bastilla au pigeon" as menu items, Google indexes those as content on your profile. Customers searching for specific dishes may find you directly. Add:

  • Every category (starters, mains, desserts, drinks)
  • Item name in French and Arabic
  • Description (2–3 sentences per item)
  • Price
  • Photo for every item (if possible)

Photos: The #1 Factor in Restaurant Click-Through Rate

Customers decide whether to visit based on photos before they even read your reviews. Poor photos = no visits. Prioritize:

Hero photo (thumbnail): This is the first image searchers see. Make it your best dish or your most photogenic dining moment. Natural light, close-up, vibrant colors. This single photo determines whether someone clicks or scrolls past.

Full menu photos: One photo per dish. Consistency matters — use the same lighting setup for all food shots. Good food photography on a smartphone is achievable with natural window light, a clean white plate, and the camera at 45 degrees.

Ambiance shots: Interior during evening service (shows atmosphere), exterior (helps navigation), bar/counter area if applicable.

Weekly new photos: Google rewards active profiles. Add at least 2 new photos every week — specials, seasonal dishes, full tables, events.

iPhone tip for food photos: Use Portrait mode + natural light from a window. Stand with the window to your side (not behind you). Get 30–40cm from the dish. Edit brightness +10, warmth +5, vibrance +15 in the Photos app. This beats most professional food photography in natural conditions.

Hours, Attributes, and Special Features

Keep your hours current — nothing destroys customer trust faster than showing up to a closed restaurant that Google said was open. Update hours for:

  • Regular weekly schedule
  • Ramadan hours (critical — customers specifically filter for open-during-Iftar)
  • Public holidays
  • Summer season changes

Add relevant attributes: "Dine-in", "Takeaway", "Delivery", "Accepts reservations", "Outdoor seating", "WiFi", "Family-friendly", "Halal", "Serves alcohol" (if applicable). These filter your restaurant into specific searches.

Add a Booking/Reservation Button

GBP supports booking integrations via third-party reservation systems. If you use a booking tool, link it. If not, add your WhatsApp number as a booking contact and create a Google Post with a "Reserve on WhatsApp" CTA. The booking button appears prominently in your Maps card and increases conversions significantly.

Reviews: The Reservation-Multiplier

For restaurants, reviews are more than a ranking signal — they're a direct conversion driver. A 4.7-star restaurant with 200 reviews will outbook a 4.2-star with 20 reviews, even if the food is identical. In Morocco's competitive dining scene, review management is a genuine competitive advantage.

The Perfect Moments to Ask for Reviews

Timing matters enormously. The best moment is after the customer has expressed satisfaction — not as they're paying (they're distracted) but as they're relaxing with tea, or as you thank them on the way out:

  • "Saha — hshemna bikoum! Kan yakfina tetfaddlu tbarkillah 3lina f Google — wakha?" (Darija, informal)
  • Hand them a QR code card that links directly to your review page
  • For delivery: include a note in the bag with QR code and brief thank-you message
  • WhatsApp follow-up: 2 hours after the meal, send your review link to regulars

Review Velocity Beats Total Count

A restaurant with 10 reviews this month ranks higher than one with 200 total but none in 6 months. Google weights recency. Build a system to generate 5–10 new reviews per month consistently:

  • Printed QR code on every table (stands, menu inserts)
  • QR code on receipt
  • WhatsApp quick reply with review link in your top 5 saved responses
  • Brief mention by staff during service ("We'd love your feedback on Google")

Handling Negative Reviews Like a Professional

Every Moroccan restaurant will get a bad review eventually. How you respond is visible to every future customer reading that review. The formula:

  1. Thank them for the feedback (even if it's unfair)
  2. Acknowledge the specific issue without being defensive
  3. Explain what you've done or will do to address it
  4. Invite them back with a direct offer

Example: "Merci pour votre retour, [Prénom]. Nous sommes désolés que l'attente ait été trop longue ce soir-là — un vendredi chargé que nous aurions dû mieux anticiper. Nous avons depuis ajusté notre gestion des tables. Nous vous invitons à revenir pour une expérience qui correspond à nos standards. Contactez-nous directement sur WhatsApp."

This response does three things: acknowledges the issue, shows accountability, and demonstrates you've taken action. Future readers see a professional operation, not a defensive owner.

TripAdvisor Strategy for Moroccan Restaurants

TripAdvisor remains essential for two reasons: (1) it drives direct international tourist traffic, especially in Marrakech, Fes, Agadir, and Essaouira, and (2) it serves as a high-authority citation that boosts your Google Maps ranking.

Optimizing Your TripAdvisor Profile

  • Claim your listing at tripadvisor.com/owners
  • Upload 30+ photos (same high-quality food shots from GBP)
  • Write description in English, French, and Arabic
  • Add accurate hours, price range, cuisine type, and special features
  • Link your TripAdvisor URL from your website and GBP description

Getting TripAdvisor Reviews

International guests are more likely to leave TripAdvisor reviews than locals. For tourist-heavy locations, include both your Google review link AND TripAdvisor link in your review request materials. Ask international guests specifically: "If you have a TripAdvisor account, we'd really appreciate a review — it helps travelers find us."

Delivery apps in Morocco (Glovo, InDrive Food, Yassir) are not just revenue channels — they're discovery platforms and citation sources. Appearing on them with an optimized profile creates additional signals that help your Google Maps ranking.

WhatsApp: The Moroccan Restaurant's Reservation System

In Morocco, WhatsApp is the default communication tool. Many restaurants already take reservations by WhatsApp informally — but there's a massive gap between "taking WhatsApp messages" and "having a WhatsApp reservation system."

Set Up WhatsApp Business Properly

  • Download WhatsApp Business (not regular WhatsApp)
  • Set up your business profile: name, category, description, address, website, hours
  • Create a product catalog with menu photos and prices
  • Write quick replies for common messages:
    • /reservation → reservation template asking for name, date, time, guests
    • /menu → link to your menu or catalog
    • /hours → current opening times
    • /location → Google Maps link + address
  • Set away messages for closed hours: "Thank you for your message. We're currently closed — we open at [TIME]. Reply RESERVATION to book a table."

Automate Reservation Confirmation

Advanced setup: connect WhatsApp Business API with n8n to auto-confirm reservations, send reminders 2 hours before, and request Google reviews after the visit. This runs 24/7 without staff involvement. See our guide on AI Automation for Moroccan Businesses for the technical implementation.

Your Restaurant Website: Minimal but Essential

You don't need a complex website — but you need something. A single-page restaurant website that does the following is sufficient to dramatically boost your Google Maps ranking:

  • Your full restaurant name, address, and phone in text (not just an image)
  • Embedded Google Map showing your location
  • Menu (PDF or HTML)
  • Online reservation or WhatsApp link
  • LocalBusiness + Restaurant Schema markup
  • Page load under 2 seconds on mobile

Restaurant Schema Markup

Add this JSON-LD to your website's head tag. Google uses it to confirm your business details and can display rich results (menu, hours, rating) directly in search:

<script type="application/ld+json">
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "Restaurant",
  "name": "Nom du Restaurant",
  "image": "https://votresite.ma/img/restaurant.jpg",
  "url": "https://votresite.ma",
  "telephone": "+212522000000",
  "address": {
    "@type": "PostalAddress",
    "streetAddress": "123 Avenue Hassan II",
    "addressLocality": "Casablanca",
    "addressCountry": "MA"
  },
  "servesCuisine": "Moroccan",
  "priceRange": "$$",
  "openingHoursSpecification": [
    {
      "@type": "OpeningHoursSpecification",
      "dayOfWeek": ["Monday","Tuesday","Wednesday","Thursday","Friday"],
      "opens": "12:00",
      "closes": "23:00"
    }
  ],
  "hasMenu": "https://votresite.ma/menu/"
}
</script>

Seasonal SEO: The Moroccan Restaurant Calendar

Morocco has distinct seasonal opportunities that most restaurants miss from an SEO perspective. Plan your Google Posts and website content around these peaks:

Ramadan Strategy

Ramadan is the highest-value dining period of the year for most Moroccan restaurants. Iftar and Suhoor searches spike enormously in the weeks before Ramadan. Prepare:

  • Update GBP with Ramadan-specific hours and Iftar/Suhoor indicator in attributes
  • Create a dedicated Google Post series: "Formule Iftar [Year]" with photo and price
  • Add "Iftar", "Suhoor", "Ramadan menu" to your GBP description during the period
  • Run WhatsApp bulk messages to your customer list with Iftar reservation availability

Summer Tourism Season (June–September)

For coastal cities (Agadir, Essaouira, Tangier, Al Hoceima), summer is peak international tourist season. Create English-language Google Posts and ensure your profile is fully translated in English. Update your TripAdvisor listing photos with summer/terrace shots.

Eid Al-Adha and Eid Al-Fitr

Families eat out extensively during Eid periods. Post lunch and dinner Eid specials, update hours to reflect Eid schedules, and target family-specific searches ("restaurant familial Casablanca") in your posts.

Measuring Your Restaurant's Local SEO Performance

Track these metrics monthly to confirm you're moving in the right direction:

  • GBP Insights: Monthly views, searches, clicks, calls, direction requests — visible in your GBP dashboard. Direction requests are the best proxy for actual visits driven by Maps.
  • Review growth: Total count and monthly new reviews. Are you getting at least 5–10 new reviews per month?
  • Average rating trend: Is it stable or drifting? Below 4.3 starts to hurt conversion rates.
  • Maps position: Search your primary keywords from your city in incognito. Note your position. Check 3–4 times per month.
  • WhatsApp reservation volume: Track how many reservations come via WhatsApp vs. walk-in vs. phone. The WhatsApp percentage should grow monthly as your optimization takes effect.
