Case Studies

How we approach real cybersecurity work.

The examples below are illustrative and anonymized. They show our method and the kind of outcomes our work delivers.

Demo content — these are representative scenarios, not real clients. We never publish fake company names, invented statistics, or unverifiable claims. Verified case studies will appear here as clients agree to be referenced.

Demo

Accounting firm · Casablanca

First full security assessment

Challenge: A growing firm handling sensitive financial records had never had an independent security review.

What we did: Discovery, asset inventory, and a full posture assessment across endpoints, email, and backups.

Outcome: A prioritized roadmap let leadership fix the highest-impact gaps first, starting with multi-factor authentication and backup verification.

Demo

E-commerce · Rabat

Web application security review

Challenge: An online store worried its checkout and customer accounts might be exposed.

What we did: An OWASP-aligned review of the application, authentication flow, and access controls.

Outcome: Findings were handed to the development team with clear remediation notes and a retest to confirm the fixes.

Demo

Clinic · Marrakesh

Vulnerability assessment

Challenge: A medical practice needed to know whether patient data on its network was at risk.

What we did: Internal network scanning with manual validation to remove false positives.

Outcome: A short, ranked list of real issues — mostly outdated software — with a clear patching plan.

Demo

Software company · Tangier

ISO 27001 readiness

Challenge: A startup needed to satisfy enterprise customers asking about security certification.

What we did: A gap analysis against ISO 27001 and a phased readiness roadmap.

Outcome: The team understood exactly what to build, in what order, to move toward certification.

Demo

Manufacturer · Kenitra

Security awareness training

Challenge: Repeated phishing attempts were reaching staff inboxes.

What we did: A tailored awareness session plus an optional simulated phishing test.

Outcome: Staff learned to recognize and report suspicious messages, reducing the chance of a successful attack.

Demo

Law firm · Casablanca

Penetration test

Challenge: A firm wanted assurance that confidential case files could not be reached from the outside.

What we did: A scoped, non-destructive penetration test of internet-facing systems.

Outcome: A clear attack narrative and prioritized fixes, followed by a retest to confirm closure.
