FAQ Release Notes
Is the JectarOne platform live?

The Assessment Report Builder and the free security tools are live now. The wider platform — dashboard, endpoints, alerts, API — is in active development and shown as a labeled preview only.

Does the Report Builder upload my client data?

No. It runs entirely in your browser. Findings and client data are never uploaded — they stay on your device and in the PDF you download.

Is there an agent to install?

Not yet. A collection/agent component is in design. Today the product is the browser-based Report Builder, alongside our consulting services.

What does it cost?

The Report Builder and free tools are currently free to use. Commercial pricing will be announced as the platform matures. Consulting engagements are quoted per scope.

Do you have ISO 27001 / SOC 2?

Not yet — these are goals we're working toward. We won't display a certification badge we haven't earned. See our security commitment.

How do I get early access or a demo?

Book a demo or email contact@jectar.one. Early-access users help shape what we build next.
