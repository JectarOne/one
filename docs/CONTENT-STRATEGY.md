# JectarOne — Content Strategy

## Goal

Build trust and attract organic traffic by publishing practical, honest cybersecurity content for Moroccan business owners and IT managers. Target: **1,000 monthly visitors** (Year 1 success metric).

## Principles

- **No hype, no fear-selling.** Help readers make decisions, not panic.
- **Plain language.** Written for business owners, not researchers.
- **Practical and specific to Morocco** where relevant (local context, regulations, common tools).
- **No fake statistics, logos, or testimonials.** Ever.
- **Every article ends with a relevant CTA** into a service.

## Cadence

Aim for **one practical article per week**. Quality over volume — a useful guide beats three thin posts.

## Topic pillars

1. **Web Security** — WordPress hardening, web app risks, security headers, HTTPS.
2. **Threats** — ransomware, phishing, social engineering, incident response.
3. **SME Security** — common mistakes, low-cost wins, MFA, backups.
4. **Microsoft 365 / Identity** — account security, MFA, safe configuration.
5. **Network Hardening** — segmentation, remote access, patching.
6. **Compliance** — ISO 27001, data protection (Law 09-08), security questionnaires.

## Article structure (template)

1. Breadcrumb + category chip + title.
2. Short intro framing the problem for a business owner.
3. Numbered, scannable sections with practical steps.
4. A callout box with the "quick win" / key takeaway.
5. CTA into the most relevant service.

## Distribution
