# JectarOne — Service Catalog

This catalog describes each service: the problem it solves, our methodology, what the client receives, and a typical timeline. All engagements are scoped to the client's size and budget and delivered under a non-disclosure agreement.

---

## 1. Security Assessment

**Problem.** Most SMEs grew their IT piece by piece and have no full picture of what is exposed.

**Methodology.** Discovery → asset inventory → posture review across endpoints, email, network, backups, and identity → risk ranking.

**Deliverables.**
- Executive summary for leadership
- Prioritized risk register
- Asset inventory of systems in scope
- Practical remediation roadmap

**Typical timeline.** 1–2 weeks.

---

## 2. Vulnerability Assessment

**Problem.** Unknown weaknesses across servers, endpoints, websites, and networks.

**Methodology.** Scope & asset discovery → authenticated and unauthenticated scanning → manual validation to remove false positives → risk-ranked reporting.

**Deliverables.**
- Technical findings with evidence
- CVSS-based severity ratings
- Step-by-step remediation guidance

**Typical timeline.** ~1 week.

---

## 3. Penetration Testing

**Problem.** Uncertainty about what an attacker could actually reach.

**Methodology.** Agreed rules of engagement & scope → reconnaissance → exploitation → post-exploitation impact analysis. Safe, non-destructive testing only.

**Deliverables.**
- Attack narrative with proof of concept
- Business-impact assessment
- Prioritized fixes + a retest

**Typical timeline.** 1–3 weeks.

---

## 4. Web Application Security Review

**Problem.** Public websites and portals are the most exposed part of a business.

**Methodology.** OWASP-aligned review of the application, authentication flow, and access controls.

**Deliverables.**
- OWASP-aligned findings report
- Authentication & access review
- Developer-ready remediation notes

**Typical timeline.** 1–2 weeks.

---

## 5. Security Awareness

**Problem.** Most breaches start with a single click by an untrained employee.

**Methodology.** Tailored live session (on-site or remote) covering phishing, passwords & MFA, safe data handling, and incident reporting. Optional simulated phishing test.

**Deliverables.**
- Live training session
- Materials tailored to the team and tools
- Optional phishing simulation results

**Typical timeline.** Half-day session.

---

## 6. Compliance Readiness (ISO 27001)

**Problem.** Larger customers increasingly require evidence of security maturity.

**Methodology.** Gap analysis against the standard → prioritized roadmap → policy & control guidance.

**Deliverables.**
- Compliance gap report
- Readiness checklist
- Clear next-step action plan

**Typical timeline.** 2–4 weeks.

---

## Standard deliverable formats

Every engagement concludes with consulting-grade documentation:

- Executive Summary
- Risk Matrix
- Asset Inventory
- Critical Findings (with evidence)
- Recommendations (prioritized)
- Compliance Checklist (where relevant)
