## Mission

Help Moroccan businesses improve their cybersecurity through clear assessments, actionable recommendations, and affordable security solutions.

## Vision

Become one of the leading cybersecurity companies in Morocco and North Africa by combining expert consulting with practical software products.

## What we do

We are vendor-neutral, so our recommendations are based on what a business actually needs — not what we are trying to sell. Our services:

- **Security Assessment** — full review of an organization's security posture.
- **Vulnerability Assessment** — find weaknesses across servers, endpoints, websites, and networks.
- **Penetration Testing** — controlled, real-world attack simulation with prioritized remediation.
- **Web Application Security Review** — assess web apps against common risks (OWASP).
- **Security Awareness** — train staff to resist phishing and social engineering.
- **Compliance Readiness** — prepare for ISO 27001 and security best practices.

## Who we serve

Moroccan SMEs and startups, plus organizations in healthcare, legal, financial services, manufacturing, retail / e-commerce, technology, and education.
