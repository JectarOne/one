# Security Policy

JectarOne is a cybersecurity company. We hold our own systems to the same standard we ask of our clients, and we welcome responsible disclosure of security issues.

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in the **jectar.one** website or any JectarOne system, please report it to us privately.

- **Email:** contact@jectar.one
- **Subject:** `Security Report — <short description>`
- **Phone (urgent):** +212 752-138075

Please include, where possible:

1. A clear description of the issue and its potential impact.
2. Steps to reproduce (proof of concept, requests, screenshots).
3. The affected URL, parameter, or component.
4. Any suggested remediation.

## Our commitment

- We will acknowledge your report within **3 business days**.
- We will provide an assessment and expected timeline within **10 business days**.
- We will keep you informed as we work toward a fix.
- We will credit you (with your permission) once the issue is resolved.

## Safe harbor

We will not pursue legal action against researchers who:

- Act in good faith and follow this policy.
- Avoid privacy violations, data destruction, and service disruption.
- Do not access, modify, or exfiltrate data beyond what is necessary to demonstrate the issue.
- Give us reasonable time to remediate before any public disclosure.

## Out of scope

- Denial-of-service (DoS/DDoS) testing.
- Social engineering of JectarOne staff or clients.
- Physical attacks against offices or hardware.
- Reports from automated scanners without a demonstrated, exploitable impact.
- Findings on third-party services we do not control.

## Disclosure

We support **coordinated disclosure**. Please do not publish details of a vulnerability until we have confirmed a fix is in place and agreed a timeline with you.
