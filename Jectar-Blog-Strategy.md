## Internal Linking Rules

1. Every article links to its **cluster pillar page**
2. Every article links to a **relevant service page**
3. Every article links to **2–3 related articles** (already published)
4. Every pillar page links to **all supporting articles** in that cluster
5. New articles always link back to older articles on the same topic

> This creates a web of authority that funnels link equity to your most important pages and signals topical depth to Google.
